# Time off and leave policy

## Intern leave allowance
Interns receive 1 paid leave day for every completed month of their internship. For a three-month internship, this is 3 paid leave days in total. Unused intern leave does not carry over or convert to cash when the internship ends.

## Requesting leave
Employees should submit planned leave through the HR portal at least 5 working days before the first day away. A manager reviews the request. For illness or an emergency, notify the manager as soon as practical and submit the request when able.

## Full-time employee leave
Full-time employees receive 18 paid leave days per calendar year, accrued monthly. Local law or an individual employment agreement may provide different terms; ask HR if those apply to you.

## Public holidays
Company-observed public holidays are separate from paid leave. The holiday calendar is published by HR for each work location.
