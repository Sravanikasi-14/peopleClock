# Workplace guide

## Working hours
Standard team hours are 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, Monday through Friday, unless a manager approves a different schedule. Employees should record the start and end of each work period in the HR portal.

## Remote work
Employees may work remotely with manager approval. Keep your calendar current, attend agreed team meetings, and protect company information on personal devices.

## Attendance location
The HR portal requests device location only when an employee chooses to clock in or clock out. It does not continuously track employees. If location permission is denied or unavailable, the attendance event may be recorded without coordinates and the employee should contact their manager.
